Klar — dictation overlay

Three ways to watch noise become text.

All three share one shell: 260×64 at rest, no radius, a 2px rule all the way round, and a near-opaque ground so it holds an edge on white paper, a dark IDE and a photograph alike. They differ in what the transformation looks like.

Rules
Red means live

The accent appears only while audio is being captured or a scan line is moving. Once text is in, the overlay goes grey and leaves.

Width earns itself

260 px at rest. It only grows once there are real words to show, and never past 360.

Error waits

Every other state auto-dismisses. The error stays until dismissed and names the fix: “No microphone found. Check your input device in system settings.”

Windows

Same shell, Ctrl + Space, and the 2px rule stays — it reads as deliberate against Fluent's rounded surfaces rather than unfinished.
