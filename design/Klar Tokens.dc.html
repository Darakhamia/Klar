Klar — tokens

Messy speech in, finished text out.

Built on the Modernist system: Archivo throughout, zero radius, 2px rules, one red. Klar adds one thing the system did not have — a dark theme, derived from the neutral ramp rather than invented, because this audience runs dark all day.
